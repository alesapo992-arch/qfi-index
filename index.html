🎯 The Five Zones

Each QFI value falls into one of five regimes. They describe what positioning looks like right now — not where the market will go. The action in each zone is deliberately contrarian.

⚙️ How It's Computed

The QFI is a weighted average of three independent components, each normalized to [−1, +1] and rescaled to 0–100. All data comes from public, free APIs.

🔄 Relative Strength Rotation

7-day performance of BTC & ETH versus a cluster of speculative altcoins (L1s, memes, AI tokens). Money rotating into BTC/ETH = tighter risk appetite. Money rotating into memes = late-cycle speculation.
